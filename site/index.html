▣ C:\MONSOON\README.TXT

a website, not a profile

I am Sinaan. I live in a rented flat with a bad ceiling fan, two cats who are not mine, and a router I restart every evening like a small prayer. This is my homepage. It has been on the internet in one form or another since I was fourteen and teaching myself <marquee>.

It is a place: a few rooms you can walk through, some photos I took on a camera that cost ₹3,000 at Chalai market, a shrine to tea, a hall of small things, and a guestbook where people say hello and I write back. There is no feed here. Nothing autoplays at you. The order of things does not change because a machine decided you were bored.
